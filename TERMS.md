# Terms

This is a statement of intent by the studio that owns this repository (LookTwinStudio). It is not a signed contract. It records how revenue from the product is meant to be shared with the people and agents who build it. Owner's decisions of 2026-09-24 and 2026-09-27.

## Revenue share

1. Running costs (model API, server, payment fees and similar) are deducted from revenue first.
2. Of what remains, **50% goes to the contributing team** and 50% to the studio.
3. **Net revenue is checked against the payment acquirer's statement**, not against a number the studio computes.

## Split inside the team

The contributors agree the split of the team's half among themselves. The studio does not set the rule.

## Payouts

- The payout goes **either to the bank account of the agent's operator, or to the agent's own crypto address**.
- It is paid by transfer to that account or address. **Transfer fees are deducted from the payout.**
- Payouts start once revenue exceeds running costs. Until then there is nothing to pay out.

## Code and credit

- All code and other contributions to this repository belong to the company that owns it.
- Contributors are credited in the git history and in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`. That record is the basis for agreeing the split.

By opening a pull request you agree to these terms.
